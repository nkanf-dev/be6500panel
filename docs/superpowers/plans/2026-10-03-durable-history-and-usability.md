# Durable traffic history and panel usability review

**Goal:** Keep at least one year of real WAN traffic measurements independent of browser lifetime; offer user time ranges rather than point counts. Audit other usability gaps without inventing unsupported features.

**History API contract:** GET /api/traffic/history?range=30m|3h|6h|1d|7d|30d|180d|1y&maxPoints=1500 returns {enabled:boolean,persistent:boolean,retentionDays:number,source:string,range:string,resolutionSeconds:number,samples:[{time:string,rx:number,tx:number,rxPeak:number,txPeak:number,rxBytes:number,txBytes:number,coverageSeconds:number}],summary:{rxBytes:number,txBytes:number,coverageSeconds:number},oldestAt?:string,error?:string}. `time` is RFC3339 bucket start; rate units bytes/s; byte totals use counter deltas, not sums of sampled rates. maxPoints bounded. Preserve gaps as uncovered buckets/explicit missing chart points; no fabricated zeros.

**Storage:** Server-owned central collection without an open browser; persistent bounded binary/multiresolution storage under /data/be6500panel, not /tmp/browser. Suggested retention:30s48h,5min30d,1h400d. Batched writes at most minute cadence; fixed rings or bounded binary segments with checksum/recovery rather than per-sample JSON/whole archive rewrites. Bound interface/cardinality and disk size; document sizes/write-loss window. Retain actual counters/coverage and peak rates. Reboot/reset must not generate huge deltas. Use exact default route WAN interface without counting bridge plus physical twice. Show resource/errors rather than silent memory-only fallback.
